Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Calculator is designed to work without an account. We minimize data collection and do not sell personal information.

Calculation data

Calculation history, favorites, notes, and tags are stored locally on your device. You can clear calculation history from inside the app.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes payment and subscription information under Apple’s privacy practices. Calculator uses verified App Store transactions only to determine whether Premium features are active.

Currency conversion

The currency converter requests public reference exchange rates from the Frankfurter API. The selected currency pair is sent to retrieve a reference rate; the amount calculation is performed in the app. Currency information is for reference only and may be delayed.

Advertising and tracking

Calculator does not include an advertising SDK and does not use cross-app tracking.

Accounts and permissions

No account is required. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Calculator support page.

This policy applies to the Calculator app published by the developer of this support site.
