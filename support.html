Calculator Support

Calculator is built to work without an account. Most issues can be resolved with the steps below.

Premium purchase not showing?

  1. Confirm you are signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Calculator → Premium.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
  4. If needed, restart the app and try again.

Report a problem

Open a support ticket through the public repository issue tracker. Please include your iOS version, device model, Calculator app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not include payment details or other sensitive information.

Open a support ticket

Subscription management

Subscriptions and cancellations are managed through your Apple Account in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
