Calculator Support & Feedback

Calculator's first public edition is free to use. There is no subscription, purchase, account or payment required to calculate, save your history or use tools.

Where is my history?

Calculations and saved work stay on your device. Open History to search and reuse them. If you delete the app or erase its local data, that information may not be recoverable. Export important calculations as a CSV file before changing devices or removing the app.

Why do currency values sometimes show cached rates?

Currency conversion provides informational reference rates, not trading prices. Network access is needed to fetch updates. When new rates cannot be fetched, the app may use the most recently available cached values; check the on-screen status and do not use displayed values for settlement or financial trading decisions.

Does everything require Premium?

No. The first public edition includes Standard and Scientific calculations, custom layouts, full local history, favorites, notes and exports, practical money tools, and reference conversions at no charge. Older internal TestFlight builds may still show a Premium subscription interface; those builds are not the public free edition.

Report a bug or suggest an improvement

Feedback is optional. You can open a ticket through our public issue tracker (a GitHub account is required). Tell us the app version, device, iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are helpful but not required. Please do not upload saved calculations, financial information, Apple Account details, passwords or other sensitive content.

Report a problem or suggest a feature

If you don't use GitHub, you may also leave an honest review through the App Store once the app is publicly available. We welcome positive and critical feedback equally; reviews are never required to use the app.
